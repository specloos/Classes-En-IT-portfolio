# Lesson 01 · Writing professional emails (B1)

> MUESTRA: lección de ejemplo creada para revisar y adaptar.

**Level:** B1 · **Duration:** 60 min · **Audience:** adults and office staff

## Objectives
- Open and close a formal email correctly.
- Use polite request structures: *Could you…?*, *I would be grateful if…*
- Write a 100-word email asking for a meeting.

## 1. Warm-up (10 min)
How many emails do you write in English each week? Who to? Discuss in pairs.

## 2. Useful language (15 min)
| Purpose | Formal | Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Dear Mr Smith, | Hi Anna, |
| Reason for writing | I am writing to enquire about… | I'm writing about… |
| Request | I would be grateful if you could… | Could you…? |
| Attachment | Please find attached… | I've attached… |
| Closing | I look forward to hearing from you. | Speak soon. |
| Sign-off | Kind regards, | Best, |

## 3. Practice (20 min)
Put the email in the correct order, then rewrite it in a neutral tone.

1. Kind regards, Laura
2. Dear Mr Brown,
3. I am writing to request a meeting to review our service contract.
4. Could you let me know your availability next week?
5. I look forward to hearing from you.

## 4. Production (15 min)
Write an email (max. 100 words) to a supplier asking to change a delivery date. Swap with a partner and check: greeting, reason, request, closing.

## Answer key
2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 1
